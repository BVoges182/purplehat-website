About us

A clearer month, for the people running the business.

Purple Hat gives small businesses clear monthly management reports, a dashboard, and financial insights. The books stay in Xero or QuickBooks. We turn those records into something you can read.

What Purple Hat is

We are an accounting practice for small businesses. Each month you get management reports, a dashboard of the figures that matter, and insights written in plain language. The work sits on top of Xero and QuickBooks. Those systems stay your system of record. We do not ask you to move your ledger.

01

Monthly reports

A clear picture of the month: how the business did, where the cash went, and what changed.

02

A simple dashboard

The same story, ready when you open it. No hunting through a spreadsheet to find the number.

03

Insights you can use

A short explanation of what the figures mean, written for the owner rather than the textbook.

Why it was built

Most owners already have a ledger. What they often do not have is a calm explanation of the month. The numbers sit in Xero or QuickBooks, then arrive later as a dense report, or not at all.

Purple Hat was built for that gap. The books can stay where they are. The monthly story should still be easy to open, easy to follow, and easy to check.

Our mission

Make business finances simple and easy to understand.

A good month should be obvious. A tight month should be obvious too. You should be able to see what changed, and what is worth a closer look, without translating an accountant’s spreadsheet first.

The story

Purple Hat was founded by Benjamin Voges, a Chartered Accountant (CA(SA)). He wanted a practice where the same care that goes into the books also goes into the explanation.

The friendly hat is a small reminder that the numbers do not have to feel cold. The work behind it is still careful accounting: the month is closed, the figures are reviewed, and the report can be traced back to the ledger in Xero or QuickBooks.

If you run a small business and you want the month explained in plain language, you are in the right place.
