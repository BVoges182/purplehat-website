About

About the practice

Books kept current. Reports leadership can use.

Purple Hat is an accounting practice for small and medium enterprises. We specialize in streamlining accounting record-keeping, producing monthly management reports, and preparing annual financial statements. Work performed by our accountants is reviewed weekly by a Chartered Accountant who is your point of contact. We use AI integrations with Xero and QuickBooks to optimize the accounting function.

01

Timely close

Accounting entries are posted daily and reviewed weekly by a Chartered Accountant. We aim to close the month within one week, with reports generated on completion.

02

Clear reports

Performance, cash, and margin, written for the people running the business. Reports are tailored to your specific needs.

03

Checkable systems

Xero and QuickBooks stay the system of record. Workflows leave a trail you can check.

04

One practice

The same person closes the books and explains them.
