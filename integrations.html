AI integrations

Your website can update the books.

A booking or form on your site can become a contact and a draft invoice. You can still check it. Made for people who work from anywhere.

The delay is usually retyping. A booking stays in a calendar until someone creates the customer and the invoice by hand. We send that event into the books. A person stays responsible for the numbers.

How it works

  1. 01

    Capture on the site

    The site records the booking, name, email, and service.

  2. 02

    Check the workflow

    It matches or drafts. You can see what it did.

  3. 03

    Update the books

    The books receive the contact and a draft invoice.

Where it fits

Use this when your website already collects the booking, and someone later types it into the books.

What stays human

A person checks the numbers. Prices come from a mapped item. If something fails, the booking is held for review.
