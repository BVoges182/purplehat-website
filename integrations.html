AI integrations

Xero and QuickBooks, connected to your website.

A booking or form on your site can become a contact and a draft invoice. You can still check the record.

The delay is usually retyping. A booking stays in a calendar until someone creates the customer and the invoice by hand. AI integrations send that event to Xero or QuickBooks. The ledger stays in charge. A person stays responsible for the numbers.

How it works

  1. 01

    Capture on the site

    The site records the booking, name, email, and service.

  2. 02

    Check the workflow

    It matches or drafts. You can see what it did.

  3. 03

    Write to Xero or QuickBooks

    Xero or QuickBooks receives the contact and a draft invoice.

Where it fits

Use this when the website already collects the event, and someone later types it into Xero or QuickBooks.

What stays human

A person checks the numbers. Prices come from a mapped item. If something fails, the booking is held for review.
