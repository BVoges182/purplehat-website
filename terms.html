User and client responsibilities

You are responsible for the information you give us and for the login we give you.

  • Account details, including your name, email address, phone number, and business name, must be accurate and kept up to date.
  • The financial data you upload or connect must be the real data for that business. Tell us if you later find that a figure, file, or period was wrong.

Rules for uploading financial data and documents

Uploads and connections are how we build the dashboard, management reports, insights, and financial statements. A few rules apply to every file and every connection:

  • Upload only data and documents you have the right to give us. That includes reports, ledgers, invoices, and other records for the business on the account.
  • The file should match the business and the period it claims to cover. Do not upload another client’s records, a draft you know is discarded, or a file that mixes in someone else’s private information without a reason for the work.
  • Do not upload viruses, harmful code, or files that are not needed for the accounting work.
  • If you connect Xero or QuickBooks, you confirm that you are allowed to connect that organisation. We use the data those services make available to prepare the reports you asked for. Xero and QuickBooks stay your system of record.
  • We may refuse a file we cannot read, that does not belong on the account, or that breaks these terms. We may ask you to send a corrected file.
